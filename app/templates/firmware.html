Firmware

Upload firmware.bin from a PlatformIO build (.pio/build/harkenn-speaker/ in harkenn-speakerfirmware). Harkenn reads the version from the file. Install it on a speaker from here or from the speaker's page; the speaker downloads it over Wi-Fi, checks it and restarts. The previous version stays on the speaker as a fallback.
