People

With people's agreement, Harkenn can learn their voices and tell the assistant who is probably talking, so it can answer them personally. It's a hint, not a lock: a recording or a similar voice can fool it, and short commands are hard to tell apart, so the assistant is told never to treat it as proof of identity. Only voiceprints (numbers, not recordings) are kept, on this server; deleting a person deletes theirs.
