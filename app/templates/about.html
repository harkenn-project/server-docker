About Harkenn

Harkenn (harken: to listen) is a private voice assistant for your home: small speakers listen and answer, and this server does the thinking, on your own hardware.

It's made by Master Magic and Pip, an AI that writes code with Master Magic, together with the Harkenn contributors. Harkenn is free and open source under the MIT licence.
