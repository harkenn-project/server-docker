Media

Music, soundscapes and meditations for the speakers' SD cards. Files you add here are made card-friendly: music and meditations become MP3s at an even loudness, and soundscapes become WAVs that loop with no seam. A speaker without a card works the same and simply has none of this. See MEDIA.md.

Adding the sample media

Ten piano pieces and thirteen soundscapes, free to share (public-domain and CC0 recordings, each credited), about 135 MB from harkenn-project/samplemedia. A new library gets them once.

Music

An album plays its tracks in order, then starts over. Use ↑ and ↓ to change the order.

Soundscapes

Each loops until it's stopped, changed or its sleep timer runs out.

Meditations

Each plays once.
