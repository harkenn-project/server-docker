Modules

What Harkenn keeps for the household, so a plain chat model can remember things, keep lists, remind you and know what's on. Turn on what you'd like; each has its own page in the menu. An agent such as Hermes has its own memory and tools, so it doesn't get these in its instructions, but it can use them through Harkenn's MCP server. Personal things (memories, reminders, events) belong to the person the voiceprints on the People page recognize, or to the household.

Calendar subscriptions

Your existing calendars, read-only: paste a calendar's secret ICS address (Google Calendar: Settings, the calendar, "Secret address in iCal format"; iCloud: share the calendar publicly; Outlook: Publish a calendar, ICS). Harkenn reads them every 30 minutes.

News feeds

RSS or Atom feeds, e.g. https://www.cbc.ca/webfeed/rss/rss-topstories. "What's in the news?" reads a few headlines from these.

Morning briefing

What "good morning" covers. A group can have its own (say, no news in the kids' rooms).
