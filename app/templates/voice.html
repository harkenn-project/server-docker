Voice and assistant

How speakers understand you and answer. Everything runs inside Harkenn by default: Whisper turns speech into text and Piper speaks the reply. Models download into Harkenn's data folder the first time they're used, so the first answer takes a while. You can point either at an existing Wyoming service instead.

Text to speech
Assistant

A chat model or an agent answers what speakers hear. Leave the URL empty and speakers answer simple things themselves (time, date) and repeat the rest back.

Conversations

A conversation starts with a tap or the wake word and goes on while the speaker listens for replies. The assistant only sees recent conversations, which keeps answers quick and cheap.

Custom wake words

Besides the built-in wake words, speakers can use your own, trained with microWakeWord (a streaming .tflite model, and its .json). Choose one for a speaker on its page.
